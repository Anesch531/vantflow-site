Evidence-led SEO is a way of making search decisions in which each recommendation starts with an observable condition, explains why that condition matters, and defines how the proposed change will be checked. It replaces “trust the expert” with a chain that another person can inspect.

The evidence may come from the site, search performance, rendered pages, crawl behaviour, business constraints or a live search result. The source matters less than its relevance and interpretation. A dashboard full of numbers is not automatically evidence for a particular decision. The method asks a narrower question: what do we know that supports this action, what remains uncertain, and what result would change our view?

This is disciplined judgement rather than automation. Tools collect useful observations, but people still decide whether a finding is material, feasible and connected to the business objective. Evidence-led work makes that judgement visible.
