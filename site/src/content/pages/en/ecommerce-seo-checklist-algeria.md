An ecommerce SEO checklist for Algeria must reflect how an Algerian store actually operates. A technically tidy catalogue is useful, but it is not enough when customers switch languages, compare delivery coverage, ask whether cash on delivery is available and need confidence before placing an order. Search pages and buying pages should answer those questions without making unsupported promises.

Use this guide as a review sequence, not as a scoring system. Start with whether search engines can reach and understand the catalogue. Then check whether category and product pages communicate clearly to people using English, French, Arabic or a mixture of terms. Finally, examine the journey from a search result to a completed order request, including COD information and delivery constraints.

### What this checklist covers

The checks below cover crawlable store architecture, product and category content, Arabic-market presentation, COD journeys, structured data, performance and measurement. They do not assume that one language, payment method or platform fits every Algerian retailer. Document the store's real operating model first, then apply only the checks that match it.

## Begin with evidence, not a generic store score

Record the hostnames, language versions, catalogue size, important categories, delivery regions and order methods in scope. Keep examples of current search snippets, rendered pages and checkout or enquiry steps. This baseline makes each recommendation testable and prevents a checklist item from becoming a vague redesign request.

Where access is limited, state the limitation. A crawl can reveal internal-link and metadata conditions, but it cannot prove order completion or revenue. Analytics can show behaviour only when collection and consent are configured correctly. Separate observed conditions from hypotheses, assign an owner to each accepted action and define how the team will verify the released change.
